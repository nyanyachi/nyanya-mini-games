Game Details

About This Game

Tic-Tac-Toe is a local two-player board game played on a 3 by 3 grid. Player X goes first, then X and O alternate turns until one player completes a line or the board fills. The page detects wins and draws automatically and saves totals for X wins, O wins, and draws in this browser. It is best for quick shared play on the same device.

How to Play

  1. Player X selects an empty cell to start.
  2. Player O selects an empty cell next.
  3. Continue alternating turns.
  4. Complete three marks in a row, column, or diagonal to win.
  5. Press New Game to clear the board for another round.

Rules

  • Only empty cells can be selected.
  • Moves are blocked after a win or draw.
  • A draw occurs when the board is full and no player has a winning line.
  • X wins, O wins, and draws are saved locally in this browser.

Tips

  • Control the center when possible because it connects to the most lines.
  • Block an opponent's immediate winning line before setting up your own slower plan.
  • Corners are usually stronger than side cells early in the game.
  • Look for moves that create two threats at once.
