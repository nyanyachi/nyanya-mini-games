Game Details

About This Game

Apple Clicker is a relaxed score-building game about turning simple taps into a bigger run. Each click on the apple adds points to the current score, and upgrades make future clicks worth more. The game works well as a short break because there is no level setup or complicated menu to learn. It is mainly about timing your upgrades, watching the score grow, and trying to push past the best score saved in this browser.

How to Play

  1. Click or tap the apple to earn points.
  2. Watch the current score and points-per-click value.
  3. Buy the upgrade when you have enough points.
  4. Keep clicking with the improved value.
  5. Use Reset only when you want to restart the current run.

Rules

  • Each apple click starts at 1 point per click.
  • The upgrade costs 50 points and raises points per click by 1.
  • The best score is saved locally when the current score passes it.
  • Reset clears the current score and click value, but it does not clear the saved best score.

Tips

  • Buy an upgrade once the cost feels worth the lost current points.
  • Longer runs benefit more from higher points per click.
  • Use short sessions to chip away at a new best score.
  • Avoid resetting unless you intentionally want a fresh run.
