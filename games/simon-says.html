Game Details

About Simon Says

Simon Says is a memory sequence game with four colored buttons. Each round shows a pattern, and your goal is to repeat the exact order without making a mistake.

How to Play

  1. Press Start to begin the first sequence.
  2. Watch the buttons flash in order.
  3. Click, tap, or keyboard-activate the colors in the same order.
  4. Complete the full sequence to score and receive one extra step.
  5. After a wrong color, use Replay to start again.

Rules

  • The game uses four colors: green, red, yellow, and blue.
  • Only player input after the sequence finishes is counted.
  • Each correct completed sequence increases the score by 1.
  • A wrong color ends the game and shows the final score.
  • The best score is saved locally in this browser.

Tips

  • Say the color names quietly in your head while the sequence plays.
  • Focus on small groups of colors instead of the whole pattern at once.
  • Wait until the flashing stops before entering your answer.
  • On mobile, keep one thumb near the board so each tap stays controlled.
