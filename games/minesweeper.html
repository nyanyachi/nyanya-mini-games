Game Details

About This Game

Minesweeper is a logic puzzle about clearing a hidden minefield without revealing a mine. Each safe number tells how many mines touch that cell, including diagonals. The game offers Beginner, Intermediate, and Expert boards with different sizes and mine counts. Desktop players can reveal and flag with mouse controls, while mobile players can tap to reveal and long press to flag suspected mines.

How to Play

  1. Choose Beginner, Intermediate, or Expert.
  2. Reveal a cell to begin the timer.
  3. Use numbers to identify nearby mines.
  4. Flag cells you believe contain mines.
  5. Reveal every safe cell to win the board.

Rules

  • The first revealed cell is always safe.
  • Revealing a mine ends the game.
  • A numbered cell counts adjacent mines in all eight surrounding directions.
  • The game is won when every non-mine cell is revealed.
  • Best completion times are saved separately for each difficulty.

Tips

  • Use clear number patterns before guessing.
  • Flag only when the surrounding numbers support the mine location.
  • Open safe areas first to reveal more information.
  • On Expert, scroll the board on small screens instead of trying to shrink every cell.
