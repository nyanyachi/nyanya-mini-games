About Sliding Puzzle

Sliding Puzzle is a classic 15 puzzle played on a 4 x 4 board. The numbered tiles share one empty space, and every move slides one adjacent tile into that space.

How to Play

  1. Tap a tile next to the empty space to move it.
  2. Arrange the numbers from 1 to 15 in order.
  3. Try to solve the puzzle using as few moves as possible.
  4. Use Restart to retry the same puzzle or Shuffle to create a new one.

Rules

  • Only tiles directly above, below, left, or right of the empty space can move.
  • Invalid tile taps do not change the board or increase the move counter.
  • The timer starts after the first valid move.
  • The puzzle is complete when the board returns to 1 through 15 with the empty space last.

Tips

  • Solve one row or small section at a time instead of chasing every tile at once.
  • Use Restart when you want to practice the same shuffled layout again.
  • Watch the empty space carefully; every move is really about positioning that slot.

Saving and Controls

Your best completed result is saved in this browser. The lowest move count wins; if two results use the same number of moves, the faster time is kept. You can play with mouse, touch, or arrow keys while the board is focused.
