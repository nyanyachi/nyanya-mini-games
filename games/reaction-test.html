Game Details

About This Game

Reaction Test is a quick timing game built around one simple challenge: wait patiently, then react as soon as the signal appears. The page measures the time between the ready signal and your click or tap, then shows your latest result alongside your best saved reaction time. Because early clicks reset the round instead of counting as fast reactions, the game rewards focus as much as raw speed.

How to Play

  1. Press Start to begin a round.
  2. Wait while the game shows the waiting state.
  3. Click or tap only after the signal changes to Click now.
  4. Read your latest reaction time in milliseconds.
  5. Repeat rounds to compare attempts against your best time.

Rules

  • Clicking too early shows a Too early message and resets the round.
  • A valid reaction is recorded only after the Click now state appears.
  • Attempts and best reaction time are saved in this browser.
  • Reset Stats clears the saved best time and attempt count.

Tips

  • Keep your pointer or finger ready before starting the round.
  • Focus on waiting for the color or message change instead of guessing.
  • Try several rounds and compare consistency, not just one fast result.
  • Use a stable device surface to avoid accidental early clicks.
