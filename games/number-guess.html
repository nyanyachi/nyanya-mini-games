Game Details

About This Game

Number Guess is a compact logic game where the browser chooses a hidden number from 1 to 100. Each guess tells you whether your number is too high, too low, or correct. The challenge is to use those hints efficiently and solve the round in as few attempts as possible. Your best score represents the fewest guesses used to find the answer in this browser.

How to Play

  1. Enter a whole number from 1 to 100.
  2. Submit the guess to receive a hint.
  3. Use Too High or Too Low feedback to narrow the range.
  4. Continue until the result says Correct.
  5. Start a New Game to generate a fresh hidden number.

Rules

  • Only numbers within the 1 to 100 range are valid guesses.
  • Each valid guess increases the current attempt count.
  • Best Score saves the fewest attempts used to win a completed round.
  • Reset Statistics clears the saved best score, not the current hidden number by itself.

Tips

  • Start near the middle of the possible range.
  • After each hint, cut the remaining range down instead of guessing nearby numbers at random.
  • Track the lowest and highest possible answer in your head.
  • Use New Game when you want a fresh target and attempt count.
