Game Details

About This Game

Snake is an arcade game about steering a constantly moving snake through a grid. Food appears in empty cells, and eating it increases both score and snake length. The challenge grows as the snake becomes longer and safe paths become tighter. The game supports keyboard controls on desktop and swipe controls on touch screens, with a best score saved in this browser for future attempts.

How to Play

  1. Press Start Game to begin the countdown.
  2. Use Arrow keys, WASD, or swipe gestures to change direction.
  3. Guide the snake into food to score and grow.
  4. Avoid the walls and the snake's own body.
  5. After Game Over, use Play Again to restart with the countdown.

Rules

  • The snake moves continuously after the countdown ends.
  • Food never spawns on the snake.
  • The game ends if the snake hits a wall or collides with itself.
  • The game blocks direct reversal into the opposite direction.
  • Best score is saved locally in this browser.

Tips

  • Leave open space ahead instead of chasing food through tight turns.
  • Use the edges carefully because they can trap the snake quickly.
  • Plan a few cells ahead once the snake gets longer.
  • Make deliberate swipes; tiny touch movements are ignored to reduce accidental turns.
