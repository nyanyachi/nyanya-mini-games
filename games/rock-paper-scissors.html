Game Details

About This Game

Rock Paper Scissors is a classic quick-choice strategy game against the computer. You choose Rock, Paper, or Scissors, then the computer randomly chooses its own move. The result is decided by the familiar matchup rules and recorded as a win, loss, or draw. The page keeps running totals in this browser, so it works well for quick rematches and simple streak challenges.

How to Play

  1. Choose Rock, Paper, or Scissors with one of the buttons.
  2. Wait for the computer choice to appear.
  3. Read whether the round is a win, loss, or draw.
  4. Play more rounds to update your running record.
  5. Use Reset Statistics when you want to clear the saved totals.

Rules

  • Rock beats Scissors.
  • Scissors beats Paper.
  • Paper beats Rock.
  • Matching choices are a draw.
  • Wins, losses, and draws are saved locally in this browser.

Tips

  • Avoid picking the same move every round if you want a more varied session.
  • Watch your long-term totals instead of judging one round alone.
  • Use quick rematches to test different move patterns.
  • Reset the record when you want to start a fresh set of rounds.
