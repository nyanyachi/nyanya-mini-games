Game Details

About Connect Four

Connect Four is a two-player strategy game played on a 7-column by 6-row board. Players take turns dropping pieces into columns, and each piece falls to the lowest open cell.

How to Play

  1. Choose a column to drop your piece.
  2. Red and Yellow alternate turns on the same device.
  3. Build horizontal, vertical, or diagonal lines while blocking your opponent.
  4. The first player to connect four pieces wins.
  5. If the board fills with no winner, the game ends in a draw.

Rules

  • The board has 7 columns and 6 rows.
  • Pieces always land in the lowest empty cell of the selected column.
  • A full column cannot accept another piece.
  • Four connected pieces horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins.
  • Moves stop after a win or draw until Restart is pressed.

Tips

  • Watch the center columns because they can support more winning lines.
  • Block obvious three-in-a-row threats before building your own plan.
  • Look for diagonal setups as well as straight lines.
  • Avoid giving your opponent a winning drop on the next turn.
